Conclusion
The current CanonicalRelation.LookAhead calculation omits required EOF lookaheads.
This is reproducible with an ordinary conflict-free grammar, without TypeScript,
semantic predicates, the EBNF reader, or parser-driven lexical policies.
No engine or product source was changed during this audit.
